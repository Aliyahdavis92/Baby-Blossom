About Me

Supporting Your Pregnancy, Birth, and Postpartum Journey

Welcome! I am Samantha, and I am a birth and postpartum doula. I support families during pregnancy, birth, and the postpartum period.

My Approach

I strongly believed families deserve to have extra support throughout the birth experience whether it be from information, advocacy, or physical support

As a doula, my goal is to provide emontional, physical, and informational support while honoring families individual needs, preferences, and choices.

What to Expect

When working with me, you can expect me to be your listening ears to your concerns, help you explore your birth options, I can provide evidence-based information, advocacy, emotional support during prenatal, labor, and postpartum, and much more.

My goal is to create a calm and nonjudgmental environment where you feel comfortable expressing your needs and making informed decisions.
