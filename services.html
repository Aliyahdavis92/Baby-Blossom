Services

Doula Services

Every pregnancy is different, but still requires compassionate support. Please explore the services offered for you and your family unique journey.

Birth Doula Support

THe Birth Doula Support service includes continous emotional, physical, and informational support during the prenatal stage, labor and birth, and 2 weeks into the postpartum stage.

I will help prepare you for your birth by providing edvidence-based information to make decision to include on your personalized birht plan. I will help advocate for your wants and needs during a vulnerable time.

Postpartum Doula Support

The postpartum doula support service includes continous emotional, physical, and help with transitioning into parenthood within the first 6 weeks of the postpartum period.

Support may be emotional support, light household assistance, newborn care, newborn education, and helpin you and your family adjust to the new addition.

Childbirth Education

The childbirth education service includes providing evidence-based information to help prepare for birth. It will also include was to advocate for yourself and understanding the available options for your birth.

During this service, birth prefernces, comfort measures, questions to ask your doctor/midwife, and postpartum care will be discussed.

Addtional Services

As a Doula, it is my duty to support the family in any way I can. I offer resources that comes with oppotunities to help parents and children.

These services may include children's activities, tutoring, breastfeeding support, and family-centered events.
